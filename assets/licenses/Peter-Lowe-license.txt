<b class=red>Ad and tracking server domain list - which hostnames are included and why</b>
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=sectionhead>
Domains included in the list are:
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=section>
<ul>
<li>Highest level domains only (ie, doubleclick.net, not ad.doubleclick.net)
<li>Not only ad servers - spyware servers, hit tracking servers, and servers that enable these technologies
<li>Other nasty hostnames are included such as some malware domains etc.
<li>Checked by hand before being added when <a href="index.php#submit.php">submitted</a> by a user
<li><a href="news.php#validation">Automatically checked weekly to see if they're still valid</a>
</ul>
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=sectionhead>
Types of servers that will be listed:
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=section>
<ul>
<li><b><i>ad servers</i></b> - servers primarily used for serving up ads
<li><b><i>tracking servers</i></b> - any server that is primarily used for users' actions or enabling it
<li><b><i>malware servers</i></b> - servers contacted by viruses, spyware, or other nasty things that routinely attack and/or hijack your computer and its resources
<li><b><i>anti-adblock servers</i></b> - servers primarily used for blocking adblock technologies
<li><b><i>spam servers</i></b> - occassionally I'll add a server that is causing a huge amount of spam, but very rarely
<li><b><i>other nasty stuff</i></b> - at my discretion, anything else that negatively impacts users
</ul>

<br>
"Dedicated" here means, the primary purpose of the server.
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=sectionhead>
List integrity: hostname validation + delisting
</td></tr>

<tr><td class=section>
<li>hostnames are checked automatically every Friday at 12:00 for validity
<li>entries that for some reason fail a DNS lookup are hidden from the list
<li>entries that fail DNS lookups for 6 consecutive checks are removed from the list permanently
<li>while I make every effort to ensure that all entries in the list meet the criteria detailed above, I am fallible: if I have made a mistake, please contact me: <a href="mailto:pgl@yoyo.org">pgl@yoyo.org</a>
